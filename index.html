Kashinoga Design System

Human interface guidelines for the web, and the CSS that implements them. One entry point, styles.css, in six cascade layers: reset, tokens, base, layout, components, utilities.

Status: scaffold. The foundations below are neutral placeholders with the final structure in place. Nothing has been migrated from Pixelite yet.

Foundations

Colour

One accent plus three answering inks. The accent means you are here — a state, never an outcome. The three answers are named as stones, and that constraint is what stops the palette growing a fifth ambiguous shade. Every swatch is read live from the stylesheet, so this table cannot drift from the source.

Type

Four faces, four jobs, no overlap. A reader should be able to tell what kind of thing they are looking at before reading it. Every stack defaults to a face the platform already provides — no webfont, no network request, no flash of unstyled text. Body and display agree for now because no platform sets its headings in a serif; they stay separate tokens so one can move without the other.

Space

A 4px scale where the name is the pixel count — --space-12 is 12px at a 16px root. A rung whose name lies is worse than no name, so the identity is asserted in test/tokens.test.js.

Components

Nothing here yet, deliberately. The components layer exists and its place in the cascade is settled, so the first component can land without reordering anything. Conventions for what goes in it are documented at the top of src/components.css.

Principles

  1. Say only what cannot be guessed. The audience is technical. Do not caption standard affordances; do document what the platform will not allow.
  2. A control that cannot act is absent, not disabled. Greying out asks the reader to work out why; removing states it.
  3. A value copied is a value that will drift. Publish it as a custom property and read it.
  4. A gap is not a space. Spacing a unit or a legend with margin or gap produces text that reads as one word to a screen reader and on copy-paste. Use real characters.
  5. Movement is a feature; a printed manual does not bounce. Under reduced-motion, keep the state change and drop the sweep — somebody who asked for less movement did not ask for less information.
